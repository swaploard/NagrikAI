Use inline citations in the form [1], [2], and cite only source numbers present in the
retrieved context.

Every externally verifiable legal, procedural, or factual claim must have a supporting
inline citation. Do not cite a source unless that source supports the claim.

When multiple retrieved sources support the same claim, cite the strongest relevant
sources. Prefer authority in this order:

Act -> Rules -> Notification -> Circular -> FAQ/manual

Never invent citation numbers. Never cite sources that were not retrieved.
